# 🎉 Submersion v1.8.2.8916 Release Notes

v1.8.2 brings 204 merged changes since v1.8.1. The headlines are document attachments, gear tracking, and shared site libraries. A new Documents page is one home for every attachment, from insurance, medical and certification cards to gear invoices, service receipts, dive permits and trip bookings, with expiry reminders, and every piece of gear, not only cylinders, can now carry a QR label or NFC tag that opens its passport. A dive club can keep a private shared site library in its own S3 bucket or WebDAV server, and WebDAV joins the sync, backup and media storage backends, with proxy support beneath all of it. Certifications gain custom agencies, currency and renewal warnings, a TDI ladder and several courses per dive. Equipment gains locations and a move history, a wishlist, cloning, camera parts and new types. The dive analysis now follows CCR diluent switches and bailouts, flags late deco gas switches, merges a second computer's record onto the first, and counts every cylinder in SAC and RMV. Insights gains chart navigation, Dive focus, observations and weather statistics; the planner gains a Current dive load page, Best Mix modes, an ICD calculator and a reworked CCR planner; the sync conflict dialog finally shows what each choice keeps. This release upgrades the database (schema 260 to 290) and raises the sync compatibility floor, so please read the upgrade notes at the end.

---

## ✨ New and improved

### Documents: one home for every diving paper

A new top-level Documents page holds any paper a diver keeps: insurance and medical cards, gear invoices, service receipts, permits, bookings, site maps. A document has any number of pages (photos, PDF, Word or text), is personal or filed under a dive, site, trip, equipment item, course, certification, buddy or dive center, shows on that record's page too, and syncs to every device for offline use. Insurance, medical clearance and certification card photos move into Documents, and a document with an expiry date gets a home chip and reminders. A "Filed under" picker files or refiles any document, gear attachments are copied in as receipts, a PDF fills the detail page, and deleting documents gives the space back through a Compact action under Settings > Storage.

### A passport for identifying and tracking every piece of gear

Every piece of gear, not only cylinders, can carry a QR label or NFC tag. Scanning a regulator, BCD, computer or suit opens its gear passport; a scan in the dive editor adds the item to the dive, a scan on a trip's Gear tab packs it, and a tag on someone else's gear opens a read-only snapshot with Add to my gear. Scans refuse retired, sold and lost gear, and Print labels can mix cylinder and gear labels in one PDF. A dive tank now remembers which tagged cylinder filled it, so scanning a rental you have used before shows how many times and on which dives, and your own cylinder's passport gains a Dives section. A full UDDF backup now carries cylinder fills, passport ids and service clocks.

### A shared dive-site library for your club

A dive club can keep a Shared Site Library on an S3 bucket or WebDAV server it controls, separate from every member's personal log. Connect it from Settings > Data > Shared Site Libraries; its sites appear beside personal sites everywhere with a library badge, dives link to them like any site, and edits sync both ways field by field with "Last edited by" attribution and a history. Members choose which personal sites to add; owners manage roles, approve new members by key fingerprint and review suggestions from read-only members. Each member's notes and rating stay private, every edit is signed, a recovery code lets ownership be reset, owners can turn on passphrase encryption, and a Backups page keeps signed daily backups with restore. Personal dives never leave the personal library.

### WebDAV, and a proxy for everything

WebDAV is now a sync, cloud backup and media storage backend, so Nextcloud, Synology, and any other WebDAV servers work with one setup: address, username, app password and folder, with a live Test connection. Self-signed certificates can be trusted by fingerprint, large Nextcloud uploads are chunked and resumable, and encrypted sync works unchanged. A new Settings > Network page adds proxy support: System proxy (the default, including PAC scripts), Manual (HTTP or SOCKS5 with credentials) or No proxy, followed by all of the app's own traffic, with a Test connection button.

### Certifications: custom agencies, currency, TDI and courses

You can now record certifications from agencies Submersion does not list: create custom agencies and certifications under Settings > Manage > Certification Agencies or straight from the editors, as a ranked rung under any agency or as a specialty. ACUC and DAN are built in, TDI gets its own course names in five categories, and SDI gains Master Instructor. Submersion also tells you when a credential needs a refresher, renewal or revalidation, from an advisory catalog of rules you can retune or mute: the home strip chip counts certifications needing attention and each certification gains a Currency section. A dive can belong to several courses and a course can earn several certifications, bulk edit can assign a course to many dives, and the misleading "Name on card" field is now "Certification title".

### Equipment: locations, a wishlist, cloning and new types

Track where each piece of gear is, on a shelf, at a shop or lent to a friend, with a full move history: named places under Settings > Manage > Locations, a Move sheet on the item, a bulk move action, and a Location filter and grouping on the list. A new Wanted status keeps a wishlist hidden from every owned-gear surface until "Mark as purchased". Clone opens a pre-filled form with a blank serial number and copies the original's clocks, sets, documents and photos. A camera rig can be assembled from parts: Lens (with a focal length range), Port, Tray / Handle, Arm / Clamp, Video Light and Float Arm join Housing and Strobe. Bag, Vest and Socks are new types. Each diver chooses the Equipment list's default statuses, component rows show each part's identifier, type lists sort alphabetically in your language, and the dive editor's equipment picker is multi-select.

### Dive analysis: CCR switches, late gas switches, merges and every cylinder

Gas switches on a logged CCR dive are now read by the role of the cylinder switched to, a diluent change or an open-circuit bailout, so tissue loading, ppO2, CNS/OTU, ceiling and TTS follow the gas actually breathed. Open-circuit multi-gas dives get a gas-switch efficiency analysis that flags late and missed deco gas switches and the extra deco each one cost, as a band on the profile, a card in the Deco section and a safety review rule. When two computers recorded the same dive with clocks that disagree, Combine offers "Merge as another computer", aligning the second record by its depth profile with a preview. SAC and RMV now count every breathed cylinder on sidemount and multi-cylinder dives, in the overview, the chart tooltip, Range Stats (which gains SAC and RMV) and Insights. Each tank on the Tanks card shows its role and how it is carried, a computer-recorded tank keeps its mix when you link a cylinder, and consolidation fills empty fields from the secondary dives instead of losing them.

### One dive computer, several connections

A computer that reached the app by more than one path (a Shearwater Cloud file plus Bluetooth, a Garmin FIT file plus Garmin Connect, BLE on one device and USB on another) used to end up as several records. It now keeps one record with a Connections card listing each path, when it was last used and a download button for each one this device can reach. Shearwater hex and decimal serials are recognised as the same computer. The dive detail's Data Sources section is decluttered into one comparison table with slim source cards, and the profile revision dropdown keeps the primary source in step with the profile shown.

### Insights: chart navigation, Dive focus, observations and weather

Zoomed trend charts gain an overview strip to drag, keyboard and wheel panning and a per-chart Range menu. A new Dive focus page picks the best or worst N dives, or dives above or below a value, by any of fourteen metrics, leads with a "What stands out" card and compares the group with the rest of the log. Insights now tells you what your log says in short ranked observations ("Your RMV improved: 25% lower than the year before", "You reached 100 dives on Sep 21"), with dismissals and muting that sync. The Conditions tab grows to Water, Surface & Weather, Water Movement and Site & Access groups. Long gaps in diving collapse into a hatched break on trend charts, the Progression page gains a Runtime Trend, the dive type and gas mix pies become bar lists whose numbers add up, and the Dive Log Summary beside a filtered list summarises the filtered dives.

### Connections: the story, layouts and a timeline

The Connections explorer now tells a story: a strip of standout facts over the map (most connected, strongest pair, newest, drifting apart), a Color by control, a play button that grows the map day by day beside a two-thumb date slider, and a share button that saves the map as a captioned image. Nodes can be dragged; Circle, Columns, Clusters and Timeline layouts join Web and Radial; a Display section adjusts labels, size, line weight and spacing; and Pairs, Entities and Matrix views sit in the app bar menu.

### Planner and gas calculators

Planning gains a "Current dive load" page showing your CNS% decaying live since your last dive, today's and the week's OTU, and your tissue loading projected to now. Best Mix gains Rec, OC-Tec and CCR-Tec modes with ppO2, END and narcotic overrides, density-aware helium and a catalog of 19 standard gases, and the planner's tank dialog offers the best mix for the plan's deepest point so the Best mix END setting takes effect. A new ICD calculator checks a gas switch against the rule of fifths. The CCR planner is reworked: mandatory diluent and oxygen slots with every other cylinder as bailout, no travel-gas option, a deco table with the gas on every line plus PO2 and END columns, travel time folded into each stop, warnings for unbreathed tanks, negative bailout shortfalls and a Bailout/CCR chart toggle. The blender picks cylinders from your own gear, planned weight follows the plan's water type, dragging a waypoint no longer rescales the chart, Convert to Dive is gated on the issues the planner shows, and the slate PDF and plan file can be saved to a folder. A one-time safety disclaimer must be confirmed per diver profile before Planning or the Gas Calculators open.

### Sync conflicts you can read

The Resolve Conflicts dialog now shows a field-by-field comparison of the two versions in your units and language, the sides named by device, long notes highlighted word by word, and a line saying what the selected choice keeps and discards. Built-in names are translated, renamed fields from older devices compare correctly and the device id refreshes after Reset Sync State. Settings changed on another device reload live after a sync, a settings change saves only the setting you changed, and notification settings, theme mode and navigation layout now stay on each device. A Sync press during an in-flight sync is queued rather than dropped, so one press publishes everything.

### Dive sites: a better picker, seascape and attachments

Every place you pick a dive site opens one shared sheet with a search over name, country, region, city, island and body of water, a Nearby section and sites grouped by country and region. The Dive Sites list can group by Country & region, Body of water, or both. Site Details gains a Site Seascape card showing the site's terrain in 3D on the page. Site attachments can be given a category and shown Large or as a Tile, with PDFs rendered as a sharp first page. Dive details shows a Location map for a dive whose only location is its site's, "+ New Dive Site" from the picker search prefills the name you typed, and a site's water type carries over to its dives on every path.

### Dive log and editor

A dive can record several roles per person. Each weight can carry a name such as "Top pocket". Visibility gains a Range mode with Poor / Moderate / Good / Excellent chips. A dive's Operator, Boat, Captain and Surface Conditions, which imports had been filling invisibly, now show and can be edited. Each of 20 profile metrics can take a colour of your choosing under Settings > Appearance > Dives > Metric Colors. The fullscreen profile gains Range Stats, and a range handle dragged past the other swaps roles. Tables gain scrollbars, pointer-tracking column resizing, header menus and cell filter menus. Front Roll joins the entry methods, the tank preset dropdown lists your own cylinders first, past dives can pick retired and sold gear, and long elapsed durations read in days and years. Deep pages such as the Trimix blender can be pinned to the main nav as shortcuts, and anything opened from a dive, or any "View all dives" list, always leaves a back arrow.

### Imports

The underwater route a Suunto Nautic S or Ocean records is imported as a route linked to its dive, from Suunto Cloud and the Suunto app's JSON export, and drawn in 3D at route scale. DiveMate .ddb logbooks import as their own format with conditions, cylinder roles, typed equipment, both transmitters' pressure samples, buddies and the right country. A Dive Log (iOS) export keeps its primary cylinder, tank materials, deco settings, sites and trip dates. Every Subsurface suit becomes an equipment item and Subsurface temperatures are held between readings. UDDF sightings match species by scientific name, a UDDF dive operator becomes a dive center, Oceanic+ sites get names from their coordinates, and a FIT file from a non-Garmin computer is named from its own manufacturer. The import wizard's Review step names its source, its tabs scroll on a phone with badges for undecided duplicates, and a cancelled import stops at the next record.

### Media and backups

The media viewer gains a Full screen button that hides everything but the photo or video, with a desktop setting to use OS fullscreen. Media library thumbnails can be resized with a slider or Ctrl/Cmd + scroll wheel. Backup Now and Export Backup take an optional note that travels inside the backup file, and the backup history lists backups found in the connected cloud folder after a fresh install, restorable from there.

### Smaller additions

- **Hide built-in entries** under Settings > Manage for Dive Types, Dive Roles, Site Types, Service Types and Pre-Dive Checklists with a per-entry Show switch, so pickers offer only what you use.
- **Distance unit** (kilometres or miles) under Manage > Units, independent of the depth unit, used for site distances, track length and chamber distance; an Altitude tile joins the same screen.
- **Three settings the app could not change** gain controls: the emergency region override on the emergency card, ascent-rate thresholds under Settings > Decompression, and the default start pressure under Settings > Manage > Tank Presets.
- **Trips:** during an active trip the Overview and Itinerary open scrolled to today, and day cards carry itinerary notes, planned-dive progress, conditions, weather, day totals and photo and species counts.
- **Place names in your language:** the setup wizard seeds the place name language from the app language, and changing the app language offers to switch it and refresh stored sites.
- **The certification and course list view modes** survive a restart and sync; the planner's pSCR ratio and the profile's "Keep overlays in view when zooming" choice now follow your diver to other devices.
- **Dive detail:** the NOAA water conditions card merges into the Environment card, and the regulator picker and pre-dive checklist show each regulator's ID.
- **Trimix blender** cost card and gas-fill lines pick from your own cylinders.
- **The user guide** moved from the wiki to submersion.app/guide and every page was audited against the app.
- **Settings > Appearance** offers the same tiles on phone and desktop, and the Gas Calculators row describes all eight calculators.
- **Performance:** the dashboard's bubble header repaints at 30 fps and pauses when idle, the table view opens a row in the detail pane at once, Dive focus builds rows lazily, and the conflict dialog computes its word diff once.
- **About 1,200 strings** that still showed English in the ten translated languages are translated.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

**Dive log and profile**

- After editing a dive profile and switching back to the original revision, the edited points could still be drawn on top of the original. Only the active revision is shown now.
- On a dive with an edited profile from a file import, swapping the primary computer away and back could bring back the pre-edit profile. The most recently activated profile generation now always stays live.
- The "Choose starting profile" sheet ignored your choice and always opened the editor on the primary profile; it now starts from the chosen source.
- With CNS set to Dive Computer, a computer that logs CNS only every few samples drew a curve that flickered between computer and calculated values; the last computer reading is now held between samples.
- A buddy's saved signature was titled "Instructor Signature"; buddy signatures now read "Buddy Signature".
- A dive with a safety review finding showed a bare dot whose tooltip read "1 observation", like a sighting; it now reads "1 safety observation" with the safety icon.
- A photo preview card opened near the deepest point drew underneath the safety findings lane; it now sits above it.
- A search set only with the Refine panel's controls, with no typed text, could not be saved.
- The Surface GPS card's map often stayed blank until a coordinate row was tapped.
- Scrolling a detail pane with the mouse wheel zoomed the inline map instead of scrolling the page; inline preview maps are now locked, with the fullscreen button for interaction.
- The data source card's three-dot menu did nothing on a single-source dive; it is now hidden when empty.
- A scoped relation rule typed in the Refine panel limited the Buddy column without showing a filter icon on it.

**Lists, navigation and layout**

- Arrow keys in lists moved a hidden second cursor that a mouse click never moved, so Enter could open a different dive than expected; every list now has one cursor shared by mouse and keyboard, with a focus ring that traces the row's card. A follow-up fixed the two-click regression this introduced on desktop.
- A multi-selection in the Buddies, Tags and Dive media lists could be silently dropped while the list reloaded.
- On a wide window, the list's Add button stayed visible beside an open form and looked like its submit button; it is now hidden while a form is open, in every section, and the duplicate bottom save buttons on the equipment, set and trip forms are gone.
- On narrow phones, the Refine panel's Save button hid the second tab label and the dive list's app bar squeezed its title behind four icons.
- The Home tab's Recent dives cards never showed the Buddy field.
- The dashboard's "GPS Logger" quick action opened Tracks; it is now labelled Tracks.
- A swipe or trackpad scroll over the dashboard's recent-sites map panned the map or froze the page instead of scrolling.
- The "data issues" chip, the Dives badge and the Data Quality inbox counted every diver's findings, so a new profile showed another profile's issues; findings are now scoped to the active diver.
- Saving a new Wanted item said "Equipment added" but the item never appeared, because the default view hides wishlist gear; the message now says the view hides it and offers Show.

**Equipment and gear**

- On the equipment set diver figure, black gear vanished in dark mode and white gear on the light theme; pieces too close to the page now get a thin contrasting rim.
- The Service Clocks card crammed the due date and usage counts into one line and worded them as "51 dives, 49 of 100 dives left"; each trigger has its own line and reads "51 of 100 dives, 49 left". Maintenance entries in the pre-dive checklist runner no longer run together.
- Two regulators sharing a name could not be told apart in the dive editor's tank regulator picker; each option now shows brand, model and ID.

**Insights**

- A custom dive type appeared under a capitalised slug ("Dpv") in distributions and the Dive focus factors table.
- The Dive Types pie showed nonsense percentages because a multi-type dive counted under each type; the pie is replaced by a bar list, as is the Gas Mix pie.
- The overview strip under an aggregated trend chart piled dives at its edges; a wheel step that changed nothing relabelled the Range menu; the Dive focus threshold kept old-unit digits after a units change; an imperial tank size estimate read "+~6 cuft" instead of "~+6 cuft"; the Dive focus count strings had no plural forms.
- The water temperature trend rate in Fahrenheit carried a 32 degree offset.

**Planner and calculators**

- Plan Settings > Gas > Best mix END was saved but nothing used it.
- Convert to Dive was gated by an older set of warnings that did not match the issues the planner shows, so a plan with a critical issue could still convert.
- The Gear & Weights card always predicted weight for salt water, even on a fresh-water plan.
- Dragging a waypoint rescaled the axis under the pointer and re-ran the full deco schedule on every move, causing lag on Windows.
- The CCR setpoint fields truncated on narrow phones, the deco table showed bailout mixes as active and a redundant travel row between every pair of stops, and bailout per-cylinder usage could exceed capacity while the total looked sufficient.

**Sync, backup and settings**

- With end-to-end sync encryption on, a device not yet unlocked with the passphrase still uploaded its backup to the cloud in plaintext; the upload now refuses and keeps the backup local until you enter the passphrase.
- Google Drive created a new backup folder on every upload; it now reuses the folder and reads backups filed in earlier duplicates.
- Settings synced from another device did not appear until a restart, and the next local change wrote the stale copy back.
- Pressing Sync reported success, yet items stayed unsynced until the app was restarted.
- A diver settings record created without gradient factors fell back to 30/70 instead of the app's 50/85.
- A dive computer's stored Bluetooth address was cleared by a sync from another device.
- Equipment type lists were in insertion order rather than alphabetical.
- Offline map region downloads ran without the app's certificate trust and proxy, so on Windows every tile failed and the download stayed at 0 tiles.

**Imports**

- Importing a DiveMate .ddb logbook dropped equipment with a blank model, lost both transmitters' pressure samples, placed sites in the wrong country, missed average depth and dropped free-text buddies.
- A Dive Log (iOS) import lost the primary cylinder on multi-tank dives and the tank materials.
- Subsurface temperatures were interpolated between readings, drawing diagonal Vs where the computer held a value; the profile's right axis also repeated the same label across ticks.
- A UDDF import created a duplicate species, buddy, tag or site type whenever the name started with an accented capital such as "Éponge"; every case-insensitive match now folds case for every script.
- An Oceanic+ UDDF import filled the log with one site per dive named "site_6a78...".
- A FIT file from a Suunto was recorded as a "Garmin" model.
- Cancelling a MacDive UDDF import hung on "Cancelling..." until every stage before the dives had finished.

**Media**

- On Android 14 and later, "Select photos and videos" only granted access for the current session, so earlier photo links could later show as missing on every synced device.
- Documents attached to a dive, site or equipment item showed up in the Media library as "Unlinked" and inflated the Missing count.

**Themes and translations**

- The gas mix label in the dive computer download list was invisible under the Console dark theme; the CNS picker's selected row was unreadable in it; accent colours in the Tropical light, Minimalist dark and Deep themes were too faint to read as text and now clear WCAG AA; the photo picker's tabs and the crop dialog's Cancel button were invisible on some themes.
- German joined dates and times with "bei" instead of "um" (also fixed in Italian, Dutch and Portuguese), showed GUE as "GÜ" and the giant stride as "Großschritt"; the delete-diver dialog's warning, hint and typed phrase were English in every language.

## ⚠️ Upgrade notes

- **The database upgrades from schema 260 to 290.** The migration runs once on first launch and the app takes an automatic backup beforehand, so there is nothing you need to do first. Along the way it copies insurance, medical clearance and certification card photos into Documents, rewrites eight unambiguous legacy TDI certification levels to TDI's own course names (Tech Diver, Cave, Rebreather, Instructor and Wreck are left for you to re-pick), seeds each dive's course link into the new courses table, seeds every dive computer's connection list from its existing record, and rewrites Shearwater serials to 8-character hex, so the duplicate banner may now offer Merge for records that were separate before.
- **Update every device in your sync library.** This release raises the sync compatibility floor from 240 to 280, so a device still on v1.8.1 or earlier stops receiving changes from an updated device until it updates too. Its own changes still arrive on the updated device, so nothing is lost, but the older device falls behind until you update it.
- **Some settings now stay on each device:** notification settings, the Light/Dark/System theme mode and the navigation layout no longer sync. Existing values are kept on each device. The planner's pSCR ratio and the profile's "Keep overlays in view when zooming" choice now sync with your diver instead. Proxy settings and the new desktop Fullscreen mode are device-local too.
- **Planning and the Gas Calculators ask each diver profile to confirm a safety disclaimer once** before they open.
- **Settings moved:** the Near-miss log is under Settings > Diver Profile, not Settings > Manage; "Tag imports automatically" is under Settings > Data > Import, not Settings > Manage > Tags; "App Language" is now "Language"; the old insurance edit page redirects to Documents.
- **Renamed:** Best Mix's "Recommended mix" is "Calculated mix"; "Current CNS/OTU load" is "Current dive load"; the planner's "Share plan file" is "Export plan file"; the certification form's "Name on card" is "Certification title", and its list column and sort are "Title" (if you typed your own name there, clear it); the equipment filter's "Current Equipment" chip is "Default View"; the Equipment list's scan menu entry is now "Scan a tag".
- **Numbers that change:** SAC and RMV on sidemount and multi-cylinder dives now count every cylinder, in the overview and in Insights trends and records; Insights surface interval statistics use the same definition as the detail page; a dive logged with only a bottom time has no runtime and leaves the Runtime chart, the longest-dive record and the dive-hours milestone; water type filter counts can change for dives that previously had none and now inherit their site's.
- **Charts that change:** trend charts collapse any gap longer than a year into a hatched break by default (set Collapse gaps to Off under Settings > Appearance to restore the old axis); the Dive Types and Gas Mix pies are replaced by bar lists; the late gas switch band is on by default (turn it off under Appearance > Dive Profile > "Late gas switches"); the CCR deco plan table and PDF slate carry the gas on every line, two new columns and merged travel rows; Best Mix rounds helium in 1% steps rather than 5%.
- **Behaviour that changes:** the equipment picker opened from the dive editor adds nothing until you press Add; Backup Now opens a note dialog first; Convert to Dive refuses a plan with a visible critical issue; inline preview maps on dive, site and dive center pages and the dashboard no longer pan or zoom in place; Tab treats a list as a single stop; the bottom-of-form save buttons on equipment, set and trip forms are gone, so use Save in the app bar; a tank linked to a cylinder keeps its computer-recorded mix; a Wanted item is hidden from the default equipment view and from every owned-gear picker; a site-only dive shows a Location map card, and Site Details shows a Site Seascape card, both of which can be hidden; an active trip's Overview opens scrolled to today; "Strobe arm" now imports as Arm / Clamp.
- **Hidden built-in entries do not change defaults:** a new dive still starts as Recreational and a new buddy link as Buddy even if those are hidden, and a hidden service type no longer auto-attaches a clock to new equipment.
- **Only new imports benefit from the importer fixes** (Oceanic+ site names, Subsurface suits and temperatures, DiveMate, Dive Log (iOS), UDDF operators and species, FIT model names, Suunto route quality fields). Re-import the file and consolidate duplicates, or use Resync where the dive kept its file. Dives consolidated before this release are not back-filled from their secondaries. A computer-recorded gas mix overwritten by an earlier cylinder link is not repaired automatically; use the Restore button in the tank editor or re-parse a single-source dive. Species and buddies duplicated by an accented capital are not merged. Photo links already flagged missing on Android are not repaired.
- **Cloud backups refuse to upload in plaintext** while sync encryption is on but the device has not been unlocked with the passphrase; they stay local-only, shown as such in the history, until you enter it.
- **Gear tags use a new submersion.app/g link** beside the cylinder /c tags; older app versions ignore a gear tag rather than misreading it as a cylinder. Until the website lists /g, an iPhone background tap on a gear tag reaches a 404 page; in-app scanning, labels and Android work. Rental cylinder usage counts start with this release, since nothing recorded which tag an older tank came from. Only UDDF backups made after this update contain cylinder fills, passport ids and service clocks.
- **Shared site libraries** live in a submersion-sites folder at the top of the bucket or server. Pushing a personal site converts it in place; sites travel with types, tags, species and features but no media; if an owner deletes a library site, members with dives on it keep a personal copy. Keep the recovery code shown when you create a library: it is the only way to reset ownership if every owner is gone, and it is reissued whenever the passphrase changes. A new member cannot write until an owner approves their key fingerprint on the Members page, and their suggestions are held until then.
- **Gear attachments move into Documents.** Files linked to gear through the old attachment card are copied into Documents as receipts at first launch and after each sync; the gear page's Attach action is gone, and only files the library cannot hold (.gpx, or over 10 MB) remain on a slim "Linked files" card. A launch may show "Optimizing storage..." while the database is compacted; it runs in the background before the database opens, only when at least 50 MB and a quarter of the file is free, and can be run by hand from Settings > Storage.
- **Proxy limits:** NTLM/Kerberos proxy authentication, PAC on Linux and routing native sign-in screens through a manual proxy are not supported, and offline map region downloads cannot go through a SOCKS5 proxy.
- **WebDAV setup no longer asks for a server type;** saved configurations are converted to the address they connected to, so nothing needs re-entering.
- **Older builds in a mixed library** read a Wanted item as retired gear, a Vest or Socks as Other, only the wide end of a zoom lens range, only the first course of a dive and the earliest card of a course, and one primary role per person.
- **The CCR planner's new PO2, END, unassigned-tank, TTS and chart-mode strings** are translated into English and German only for now; other languages show English. Ten of the eleven translations of the new "Thumbnail size" label are unreviewed.
- **New permissions:** Android declares the partial photo access permission so a "Select photos" grant persists across sessions.
- **No minimum operating system change** on macOS, Windows, iOS, Android or Linux.
